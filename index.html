SOKKIA iM-105 Total Station Operation Panel Simulator

About the Sokkia iM-105 Total Station Simulator

The Sokkia iM-105 Total Station Simulator is an interactive web-based engineering and surveying instrument created by Eyad Mohamed Mekky. This tool accurately models the physical faceplate buttons, dot-matrix LCD screen, and internal onboard software of the Sokkia iM-100 Series (iM-101, iM-102, iM-103, iM-105) manufactured by Topcon Positioning Systems.

Key Surveying Software Capabilities

  • Observation Mode (OBS): Real-time display of Slope Distance (SD), Horizontal Distance (HD), Vertical Distance (VD), Zenith Angle (ZA), and Horizontal Angle Right (HA-R) across 3 configurable pages.
  • Dual-Axis Tilt Compensator: Graphical circular and tubular spirit levels with dynamic arc-second tilt angle monitoring.
  • Electronic Distance Measurement (EDM): Fast and accurate distance measurement with Prism (-30mm offset), Reflective Sheet (0mm offset), and Reflectorless modes with atmospheric ppm correction.
  • Coordinate Measurement (COORD): 3D coordinate calculation (North, East, Elevation) with instrument height and prism target height adjustments.
  • Free Station Resection: 2-point resection algorithm solving occupied station coordinates and standard deviation residuals.
  • Stake-Out (S-O): Guidance display for stake-out angle (dHA) and distance (dHD) with real-time acoustic pitch beeping.
  • Missing Line Measurement (MLM): Baseline-to-target distance, slope, and grade computation.
  • Area & Perimeter: Polygon area calculation in square meters and square feet with perimeter summation.
  • Remote Elevation Measurement (REM): Overhead height triangulation for power cables and overhead structures.

Educational Target Audience

Designed specifically for civil engineering university students, surveying professors, geomatics researchers, and land surveying field crews for laboratory practice and equipment familiarization.
